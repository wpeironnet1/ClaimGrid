# Legal and safety guardrails

- ClaimGrid provides research and workflow tools, not legal, surveying, geological, or title advice.
- Federal land management is not the same as land being open to mineral entry.
- A user must investigate withdrawals, mineral ownership, prior claims, on-ground monuments, access rules, and state/county requirements.
- Physical discovery and location requirements cannot be completed by tapping a map.
- Monument standards vary. The app must not describe a universal “three-foot pole system.” It should load current state-specific requirements and show official citations.
- BLM recording generally must occur within 90 days of location, while state/county deadlines may be shorter.
- Field mode should warn about private property, tribal land, closures, hazards, and environmental restrictions.
- Community posts must not reveal sensitive archaeological sites or encourage trespass, unsafe entry, or unlawful disturbance.
