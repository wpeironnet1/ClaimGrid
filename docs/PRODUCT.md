# Product brief

## Audience

Prospectors, recreational miners, precious-metals enthusiasts, small operators, and newcomers who need a legible path through land research, field requirements, and recordkeeping.

## Core jobs

1. Identify areas worth researching.
2. Understand why a parcel may or may not be open to mineral entry.
3. Preserve the sources and dates used in that research.
4. Prepare a state-specific field checklist.
5. Capture monuments, coordinates, discovery notes, and evidence on site.
6. Prepare county and BLM filing information without implying legal approval.
7. Track deadlines and annual obligations.

## Monetization

Free includes education, basic map layers, and one research area. Pro is designed to include unlimited projects, saved source snapshots, offline field mode, document generation, filing deadline reminders, exports, and monitored-area alerts. Community content should not be pay-to-rank.

## Trust language

Never label land simply “available.” Prefer: “No active claim shown in the selected source,” followed by source date, data-quality caveats, and required verification steps.
